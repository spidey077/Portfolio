About Myself

Your website should win clients, not lose them.

I'm Muhammad Imdadullah, a full-stack developer who helps service businesses and online stores get more inquiries from their websites. I rebuild outdated, slow, or broken sites into fast, modern ones, add AI chatbots that capture leads around the clock, and set up automatic follow-ups so no inquiry goes cold. No website yet? I build your first one from scratch. I focus on healthcare practices, cleaning companies, car detailing businesses and e-commerce brands, using React, Next.js and Tailwind CSS.
