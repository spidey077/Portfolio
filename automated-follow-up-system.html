Automated Lead Follow-Up System

Overview

Slow follow-ups quietly cost agencies and businesses qualified opportunities. This automated lead follow-up system keeps every prospect moving through a thoughtful sequence without relying on manual reminders or spreadsheets.

Built with two modular n8n workflows, it uses the initial conversation to draft personalized emails, calculates the right follow-up intervals, logs every touchpoint, and stops outreach as soon as a prospect replies.

Key Features
  • Outreach Engine: Checks the leads sheet daily and identifies prospects due for their next follow-up.
  • Precise Follow-Up Timing: Applies 24-hour, 48-hour, and later follow-up intervals consistently.
  • AI Personalization: Drafts relevant emails from the original lead conversation and campaign context.
  • Automatic Logging: Records message status, timestamps, and touchpoints in Google Sheets.
  • Reply Monitor: Watches for incoming replies in near real-time and updates the lead record immediately.
  • Sequence Suppression: Stops future emails when a prospect responds, preventing awkward duplicate outreach.
Use Cases
  • Agencies following up with new sales leads
  • Service businesses managing inbound inquiries
  • Sales teams that need consistent outreach without manual tracking
  • Automation agencies deploying repeatable client pipelines
Tech Stack

n8n Workflow Automation, OpenAI API, Gmail API, Google Sheets API
