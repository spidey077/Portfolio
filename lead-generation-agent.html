Autonomous AI Lead Generation Agent

Overview

Imagine waking up to qualified leads in your inbox without spending a single minute prospecting. Running an agency or service business means outreach costs hours of manual searching, auditing, writing personalized messages, and tracking contact history daily.

I built a fully automated system that runs entirely on autopilot. It finds targeted prospects, audits their performance, filters out unqualified leads, sends tailored outreach, and logs all campaign data automatically—freeing up hours to focus on closing deals and serving clients.

Key Features

  • Automated Prospect Discovery: Targets specific business categories by industry and location.
  • Real-Time Technical Auditing: Checks target websites for speed, mobile optimization, SEO, and security metrics to verify actual need.
  • Smart Triage & Suppression: Automatically skips leads that don't need services and prevents duplicate outreach.
  • Hyper-Personalized Outreach: Craft non-jargon, tailored emails dynamically generated based on specific audit findings.
  • Complete Pipeline Tracking: Automatically logs status updates across all leads (sent, skipped, pending, failed).
  • Scheduled Execution: Runs multiple times per day to continuously deliver fresh, uncontacted leads.

Use Cases

This automation engine can be adapted for any outreach-driven offer, including:

  • Web development and design agencies
  • SEO and digital marketing firms
  • B2B consulting practices
  • SaaS and specialized service providers

Tech Stack

n8n Workflow Automation, OpenAI API (LLM Reasoning), Google Sheets API, Custom Web Scraper & Audit Engine
